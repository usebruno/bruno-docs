Bruno supports adding custom Certificate Authority (CA) and client certificates to your requests if required by the API you are working with.

## Try it out

Explore the [client-cert](https://github.com/bruno-collections/client-cert-badssl) sample collection to practice custom CA trust and client certificates.

  collectionUrl="https://github.com/bruno-collections/client-cert-badssl"

### Adding a custom CA certificate

Navigate to the `Preferences` menu in Bruno and select `Use Custom CA Certificate`, then upload the selected file.

![Custom CA Certificate](/images/screenshots/auth/customcacert.webp)

### Adding client certificates to Collections

You can add and manage client certificate(s) on any Collection by going to the `Collection Settings` menu and selecting `Client Certificates`.

Simply enter the domain, designate the type of certificate, and then upload the relevant file(s).

![Client Certificate](/images/screenshots/auth/clientcerts.webp)

<Info>
  From Bruno **v4.1.0**, you can also add client certificates at the **app (Preferences) level**, so the same certificate can be reused across collections.
</Info>
