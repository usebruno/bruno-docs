[1Password](https://1password.com/) stores credentials in vaults as items and fields. Bruno integrates with 1Password so you can resolve `op://vault/item/field` references in requests without copying secret values into environment files or git.

# 1Password Secret Manager

<Info>
  **How Bruno keeps 1Password secrets safe**

  - Secret values are **never saved on your machine**. Bruno only keeps the account name, Service Account token configuration, and non-secret `op://` references.
  - Secrets are fetched **when you need them** (Fetch Secrets or on send) and kept in memory for that environment for the rest of the session. Later requests reuse them; a manual refresh pulls fresh values.
  - Closing Bruno or the collection clears those values. Reopen the app and Bruno fetches secrets again on demand. Interpolation in requests keeps working as usual.
</Info>
