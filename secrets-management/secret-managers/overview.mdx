# Integration with Secret managers <PremiumBadge noLink />

For users of Bruno Ultimate, you can take your secret management one step further and perform an integration with a secret vault.

<Info>
  **External secrets stay in memory, never on disk**

  - Secret values are **never written to disk** - only provider config and secret identifiers are saved.
  - Secrets are fetched **on demand** into a per-environment, in-memory cache for the active session.
  - The cache clears when you close the app. Reopen Bruno and it will fetch secrets on demand.
</Info>

Currently, we support integrations with:

- [HashiCorp Vault](/secrets-management/secret-managers/hashicorp-vault/overview)
- [AWS Secrets Manager](/secrets-management/secret-managers/aws-secrets-manager/overview)
- [Azure Key Vault](/secrets-management/secret-managers/azure-key-vault/overview)
- [Google Cloud Secret Manager](/secrets-management/secret-managers/google-secret-manager/overview)
